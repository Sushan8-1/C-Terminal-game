Survival Adventure Game

Survival Adventure Game is a C++ terminal-based RPG where the player explores different areas, fights enemies, earns gold, upgrades stats, and tries to survive as long as possible.

The game starts by asking the player to enter a name. After that, the player is placed into a menu-based system where they can choose different actions like exploring a forest, visiting a shop, entering a dungeon, or fighting in an arena. Each choice leads to different outcomes, including battles, rewards, or random events.

The combat system is turn-based. The player and enemy take turns dealing damage until one of them is defeated. Winning battles gives gold and experience points. As the player gains experience, they level up, which increases their attack, defense, and resets health.

The shop allows the player to spend gold on potions or upgrades to improve their chances in future battles. The forest and dungeon contain random encounters, including enemies and rewards, making each playthrough slightly different.

The game continues until the player either chooses to exit or their health reaches zero.

This project demonstrates the use of object-oriented programming in C++, including classes, functions, and basic game logic systems like combat, leveling, and random events.
